02Goals

The two goals

Everything we do as pod leads serves two goals.

01

Produce high-quality data, efficiently

The output of every pod is data our customers build their models on, so accuracy and consistency are the bar. We hit it through strong communication, solid training, early issue spotting, and tight feedback loops — quality and speed together, not one at the cost of the other.

02

Identify, retain & promote top contributors

Our contributors are the engine of the business. Spot strong performers early, keep them engaged and growing, and open paths to bigger responsibility — turning the contributor base into a true growth engine. The ambition: the best contributor experience anywhere.

03Values

How we work

The principles that guide every pod.

i.

Communication is the experience

Be responsive and clear, and flag changes early. Communication isn't added to the work — it is the work.

ii.

Earn contributor trust

Trust is built in every interaction: each answer given, each issue resolved, each payment made right.

iii.

Own your pod

You decide how your pod runs. Experiment freely, then share what works and what doesn't with the team.

iv.

Pods move as one

We're building the ship while we sail it. We share openly, treat feedback as a gift, and rise as one fleet.

About the role

Our work gets done in pods — small teams of contributors working a project together. As a Pod Lead, you are a player-coach: you do the work alongside your pod and lead it. You set the standard by example, you're the first person your contributors turn to, and you're ultimately accountable for your pod's success.

That means leading from the front on the three things that matter most — quality, hours, and retention — while building a pod that people actually want to be part of. If you like rolling up your sleeves on real tasks and turning a group of individuals into a high-performing, connected team, this role is for you.

What you'll own

  • Lead by example. Work tasks alongside your pod and set the bar for quality, throughput, and consistency. Your own output is the standard the pod measures against.
  • Be the first line of support. Serve as the first point of contact for your contributors — answer questions, resolve blockers fast, and keep the work flowing. Escalate to ops only what truly needs it.
  • Drive quality. Review work against project guidelines, give clear and constructive feedback, and coach contributors toward stronger, more consistent output.
  • Drive hours and throughput. Keep your pod active and on pace, monitor capacity and deadlines, and make sure committed hours translate into completed work.
  • Drive retention. Spot at-risk contributors early, understand why people stay or leave, and take action to keep your best people engaged and growing.
  • Build community. Create a pod that feels supported and connected — run regular check-ins, recognize good work, and foster a culture people want to stick with.
  • Advocate for fixes. Surface recurring friction, unclear guidelines, and tooling or process problems to the ops team, and push for the changes that make your pod's work better.
  • Own pod success. Track the metrics that matter (quality scores, completion rates, active hours, retention) and take ultimate responsibility for how your pod performs.

What we're looking for

  • Experience leading or coordinating a team, group, or project — you're comfortable being the person others rely on.
  • Willingness to be hands-on: you're happy doing the work, not just overseeing it.
  • Strong communication and people skills; you can give feedback that lands and keep morale high remotely.
  • A quality- and process-oriented mindset with sharp attention to detail.
  • Comfort working with metrics to spot problems and track progress.
  • Calm, dependable problem-solving in a fast-changing, early-stage environment.
  • Strong async habits and availability that overlaps with your pod's working hours.

Workada is an equal opportunity organization. We welcome contractors of all backgrounds.
